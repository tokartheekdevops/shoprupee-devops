Rolling Update

New Pods are introduced gradually while old Pods are replaced. This reduces downtime during an application update.

OLD POD → NEW POD
🚧

Taint

Worker 1 is protected with a taint so unrelated Pods are prevented from scheduling onto the dedicated Nginx node.

WORKER 1 ⊣ UNMATCHED POD
🎟️

Toleration

ShopRupee has a matching toleration, allowing its Pods to run on the tainted Nginx Worker 1.

TAINT ↔ TOLERATION
🎯

NodeSelector

NodeSelector specifically selects the node having the label dedicated=nginx.

dedicated=nginx → WORKER 1

🎯 ShopRupee Pod Scheduling
